## 1. Google Maps — our own headless browser (CONFIRMED)

Setup: `playwright-core@1.63.0`, cached Chromium `D:\Dev-Cache\ms-playwright\chromium-1243`, headless, locale en-US,
timezone America/Chicago, normal desktop Chrome user-agent, URL
`https://www.google.com/maps/search/<query>/?hl=en&gl=us`.

- No consent wall, no "unusual traffic" page, no CAPTCHA on 2 searches + 4 place pages.
- The results list is `div[role="feed"]`; scrolling it loads more; it ends with "You've reached the end of the list".
  - "house painter in Austin, TX" → **120 cards** (the cap), reached end after 14 scrolls.
  - "house painter in San Diego, CA" → **82 cards**.
  - So one search never returns more than 120 → statewide coverage needs many small-area searches.
- Card data (list view): name (`a[href*="/maps/place/"]` aria-label), rating + review count
  (`span[role="img"]` aria-label e.g. `"4.8 stars 1,083 Reviews"`) on **118/120** Austin cards, category + street
  address line (`Painter · 12357 Riata Trace Pkwy`), phone text, a "Website" button.
  - San Diego: 82 cards — phone on 80, Website button on 78, street address on 58, **24 (29%) with no street address**
    (service-area businesses hide it).
  - The place link carries the Google place id (`!19sChIJ…`), CID hex (`!1s0x…:0x…`) and coordinates (`!3d…!4d…`).
- Place page (`<place link>?hl=en&gl=us`) gives:
  - phone: `button[data-item-id^="phone:tel:"]` → data-item-id `phone:tel:+15123730002` (E.164) and aria-label
    `Phone: (512) 373-0002`
  - website: `a[data-item-id="authority"]` href (e.g. `https://that1painter.com/austin` — may be a location sub-page)
  - full address: `button[data-item-id="address"]` aria-label `Address: 12357 Riata Trace Pkwy, Austin, TX 78727`
  - rating `span[role="img"][aria-label*="star"]` (`4.8 stars`), review count in aria-labels (`1,083 reviews`), star
    histogram (`5 stars, 1,004 reviews`), category button text (`Painter`).
- The words "limited view" exist somewhere in the place-page body text even though the page was complete (rating,
  reviews, phone, website all shown) → detecting limited view by text match is WRONG; detect it by the
  absence of the review count / review tab instead. (Feb 2026 reports: Google's limited view hides review counts for
  flagged traffic without any error page — a blank must never be stored as 0.)
- Timing: one full search (scroll to end, 120 cards) + 2 place pages ≈ 30–36 s.
- Category noise in the San Diego list: Painter 72, Painting 6, Wallpaper installer 1, Interior designer 1 → a
  category filter is needed. Franchise locations appear (CertaPro, Five Star Painting).

## 2. California contractor board (CSLB) free download (CONFIRMED, with a download problem)

Page: `https://web.cslb.ca.gov/Onlineservices/DataPortal/ContractorList` — ASP.NET postback: select
`#MainContent_ddlStatus` = `M` (License Master) / `P` (Personnel) / `W` (Workers' comp), then click
`#MainContent_lbMasterCSV` / `#MainContent_lbtnPersonnelcsv` (Excel variants exist). Free, no login, no CAPTCHA.

- **License master** (78.2 MB CSV, "Updated as of 10/23/2026"): 245,633 licences (renewed or renewable). Columns:
  `LicenseNo, LastUpdate, BusinessName, BUS-NAME-2, FullBusinessName, MailingAddress, City, State, County, ZIPCode,
  country, BusinessPhone, BusinessType, IssueDate, ReissueDate, ExpirationDate, …, PrimaryStatus, SecondaryStatus,
  Classifications(s), …` (classification string like `C33| C43`).
  - C-33 (Painting & Decorating): **16,350** licences; **15,555 CLEAR** (active); 11,986 CLEAR with C-33 as the only
    class. Business types: Sole Owner 9,566 · Corporation 5,907 · LLC 537 · Partnership 327.
  - Phone present on 16,339 of 16,350.
  - **Sole owners**: the licence name IS the person (`DOCKERY RANDALL MARK` / FullBusinessName
    `RANDALL MARK DOCKERY`) → owner name straight from the master file.
  - Top counties (CLEAR C-33): Los Angeles 2,835 · San Diego 1,407 · Orange 1,380 · Riverside 986.
- **Personnel file** (names + titles per licence: `LIC-NO, LastUpdated, REC-TP, SEQ-NO, Name-TP, Name,
  EMP-Titl-CDE, CL-CDE, CL-CDE-STAT, ASSN-DT, DIS-ASSN-DT, …`; name fixed-width `LAST  FIRST  MIDDLE`; multiple
  roles pipe-separated with association/dis-association dates). Titles seen: Sole Owner, Responsible Managing
  Officer/CEO/President, Officer, RMO, RME, CEO/President, Qualifying Partner, General Partner, LLC Member, Deceased.
  - ⚠️ **The download arrived TRUNCATED mid-row on all 3 attempts** from this connection: 45.8 MB / 202,665 lines
    (last licence 962063), then 14.4 MB / 60,600 lines, then the Excel option 11.8 MB after 150 s. Licences above
    ~962,000 (everything issued in roughly the last 15 years) were missing → looks like a server-side time limit on a
    slow link. Plan: download from a fast server (Railway/CI) and VALIDATE completeness (last row parses, highest
    licence number ≈ the master's 1,163,406), or fall back to the per-licence lookup (Instant License Check) for
    matched licences only.
- **Match test** (San Diego "house painter" search, 82 cards): matching the Google phone (last 10 digits) to the
  CSLB `BusinessPhone` alone linked **37 listings**, 36 of them to a C-33 licence (10 sole owners). Name + address
  matching should raise this; Google phones are sometimes call-tracking numbers.
- No emails in CSLB data (Business & Professions Code §27).
